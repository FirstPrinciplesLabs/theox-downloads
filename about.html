About TheoX

A workspace for scientific discovery.

TheoX brings a scientist, their research materials, computational tools, and AI collaborators into one durable workspace. It is designed for the full life of a question: observing a pattern, forming a conjecture, trying to break it, proving what survives, and writing the result.

The basic unit

Everything belongs to a Study

A Study is a living research workspace. It can contain data, definitions, code, literature, simulations, visualizations, conjectures, open problems, counterexamples, proofs, and a manuscript.

Instead of losing context across chats and files, TheoX keeps the history of the investigation connected. A researcher can inspect how an idea changed, which attacks it survived, and what evidence supports its current status.

How it works

Conversation backed by real research tools

  1. 01

    Build the research environment

    Describe an area or bring existing data. TheoX can organize the Study, write computation code, inspect files, search literature, and create the structures needed for the work.

  2. 02

    Generate ideas from several directions

    AI reasoning works alongside deterministic conjecture search, computation, visualization, simulation, and concept invention. Each route can expose patterns the others miss.

  3. 03

    Apply adversarial pressure

    Candidate statements are challenged with counterexample search, proof attempts, known-result checks, out-of-sample examples, and specialist review. Failed attempts remain useful evidence.

  4. 04

    Preserve and communicate the result

    The Study records what changed and why. Researchers can inspect its scientific history, collaborate with others, and develop a living LaTeX manuscript beside the evidence.

Scientific discipline

Generation is not verification

TheoX can propose bold conjectures and problems, but a generated statement is not automatically true, novel, or open. Those are evidence-backed statuses, not presentation labels.

The system is built to preserve provenance and uncertainty: what was computed, which literature was checked, what agents attempted, which examples were added, and whether a proof was formally verified.

Why TheoX

Make the research process cumulative

Scientific work rarely follows a straight line. Productive failures, new definitions, surprising plots, and partial arguments often matter as much as the final theorem. TheoX turns that evolving process into a research object that humans and AI systems can continue rather than repeatedly reconstruct.

Current release

TheoX is in internal preview

The present build is for First Principles testing on Apple silicon Macs. It is an active research product, and its workflows will continue to change as they are exercised on real Studies.
