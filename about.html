About TheoX

Science should remember how it arrived.

TheoX gives people and AI a shared place to ask questions, write code, inspect evidence, challenge ideas, and develop results without losing the history that made them possible.

The research object

A Study becomes a living record

A Study can hold data, definitions, code, literature, simulations, visualizations, conjectures, problems, counterexamples, proofs, conversations, and manuscripts.

Together these form a Dynamic Research Object: a versioned history with authorship, licenses, evidence, and lineage. Publications are durable views of that history. Replications begin from an exact release and add their own evidence.

How it works

One continuous research environment

  1. 01

    Work from the question

    Begin with an idea, an existing dataset, or a body of work. TheoX can inspect the Study, write and run code, search literature, and shape the environment around the research.

  2. 02

    Discover across modes

    AI reasoning works alongside deterministic search, computation, visualization, simulation, and concept invention. Each mode can reveal structure the others miss.

  3. 03

    Challenge every claim

    Ideas face counterexample search, proof attempts, literature checks, out-of-sample examples, and specialist review. Failures remain part of the scientific record.

  4. 04

    Publish the history

    TheoX preserves what changed, who contributed, and why. Collaborators can release a paper, dataset, proof, or complete research object from the same source of truth.

Scientific discipline

Bold ideas. Explicit evidence.

TheoX can propose ambitious conjectures and problems, but truth, novelty, and openness are evidence-backed statuses rather than presentation labels.

The record preserves what was computed, which literature was checked, what collaborators attempted, which examples were added, and whether a proof was formally verified.

Why TheoX

Make discovery cumulative

Scientific work rarely follows a straight line. Productive failures, new definitions, surprising plots, and partial arguments often matter as much as the final result. TheoX lets collaborators continue that evolving object across models, institutions, and generations of the work.
